## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: The assistant is reachable from every page

The storefront SHALL offer a control in the site chrome that opens a chat panel,
on every page that uses the default layout. The control SHALL be recognisable as
a way to reach an assistant rather than as a generic messages or inbox control,
and SHALL be visually distinct from the other controls in the site chrome.
Opening or closing the panel SHALL NOT navigate the visitor away from the page
they are on, and SHALL NOT discard their cart.

A product detail page SHALL additionally offer a control that opens the same
panel with a question about that product already written into the message box.
That control SHALL obey the same rules as the one in the site chrome: it SHALL
NOT navigate the visitor away, and SHALL NOT discard their cart.

#### Scenario: Opening the panel mid-browse

- **WHEN** a visitor on a product page opens the chat panel
- **THEN** the panel opens over the page
- **AND** the page beneath it is unchanged

#### Scenario: Closing the panel

- **WHEN** a visitor closes the panel
- **THEN** they remain on the same page with their cart intact

#### Scenario: Telling the control apart from the rest of the chrome

- **WHEN** a visitor looks at the site chrome
- **THEN** the assistant control carries a mark that reads as an assistant
- **AND** no other control in the chrome uses the same mark

#### Scenario: Asking about the product being looked at

- **WHEN** a visitor on a product page uses that page's own control to open the panel
- **THEN** the panel opens over the page with the message box holding a question naming that product
- **AND** the visitor is still on the product page with their cart intact

#### Scenario: The prefilled question is the visitor's to send

- **WHEN** the panel has opened with a question already in the message box
- **THEN** no message has been sent and no provider call has been made
- **AND** the visitor may send it, edit it first, or clear it and ask something else

### Requirement: The panel opens with a greeting from the assistant

Before any message has been exchanged, the panel SHALL show a greeting written
in the assistant's own voice that greets the visitor, says what the assistant
is, names the kinds of question it can answer, and states that the visitor
confirms an order themselves. The greeting SHALL be storefront copy: it SHALL
NOT be generated by the provider, SHALL NOT cost a provider call, and SHALL NOT
enter the message history the provider is given.

The greeting SHALL be replaced by the conversation once the visitor sends a
message, and SHALL return when a visitor starts a new conversation. A question
sitting unsent in the message box is not an exchanged message, so the greeting
SHALL still be shown alongside it.

#### Scenario: Opening a panel with no conversation

- **WHEN** a visitor opens the panel and has sent no message
- **THEN** they are greeted, told what the assistant is, told the kinds of question it answers, and told that they confirm an order themselves
- **AND** no provider call was made to produce it

#### Scenario: The conversation starts

- **WHEN** the visitor sends their first message
- **THEN** the greeting is replaced by the conversation

#### Scenario: Starting again

- **WHEN** a visitor starts a new conversation after the previous one ended
- **THEN** the greeting is shown again

#### Scenario: What the provider is given

- **WHEN** the messages sent to the provider are inspected after a visitor's first question
- **THEN** the greeting is not among them

#### Scenario: A greeting beside a prefilled question

- **WHEN** the panel opens from a product page with a question already in the message box
- **THEN** the greeting is shown, because no message has been exchanged

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A prefilled question never overwrites the visitor's own words

Opening the panel with a prefilled question SHALL NOT discard a conversation
already under way, and SHALL NOT replace text the visitor has already typed into
the message box. Where the visitor has typed something, the panel SHALL open and
leave it as it is.

A conversation already under way SHALL NOT stop a question arriving. Where the
message box is empty, the question SHALL be placed in it and the conversation
SHALL continue beneath it, unchanged. A visitor who reads one product, asks
about it, then moves to another and asks about that one is the ordinary case,
not the exception.

A prefilled question SHALL be an ordinary visitor message. Nothing SHALL mark it
as machine-written to the provider, and the assistant SHALL be given no tool,
argument or instruction on account of it.

#### Scenario: A conversation is already under way

- **WHEN** a visitor who has already exchanged messages opens the panel from a product page with an empty message box
- **THEN** the new question is in the box, ready to send
- **AND** the conversation they already had is still there beneath it, unchanged

#### Scenario: The visitor has already started typing

- **WHEN** a visitor has typed into the message box, closes the panel, then opens it from a product page
- **THEN** what they typed is still there, unreplaced

#### Scenario: What the provider receives

- **WHEN** the visitor sends a prefilled question and the request to the provider is inspected
- **THEN** it carries the question as an ordinary visitor message
- **AND** the tools offered to the provider are unchanged
